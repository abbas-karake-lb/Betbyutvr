Your little world

Live: choose your resident in Household, then click a person or furniture to open its action menu. Click the ground to walk. Shift-click queues an action. Work earns $450; household bills are charged daily.

Build: choose an item and click a grid square. Rotate changes furniture direction and wall edge. Sell removes objects, walls, or flooring. Building pauses time.

Desktop: drag to orbit; scroll to zoom. Walk view: WASD / arrows to move, drag to look.

Quest: enter VR, point a controller and pull the trigger. A floating menu provides the catalog and household controls. Left stick moves; right stick turns; in tabletop view right stick up/down changes scale. Grip brings the menu in front of you. Walk mode has a teleport tool in the VR menu.

Relationships: control one resident, then click another. Build friendship through talking and jokes, then unlock hugs and adult romance. Propose, marry, and choose Try for a baby with an accessible double bed; private time fades out, and a new child arrives two game days later. Romantic actions are for unrelated adults.

Furniture access: kitchen appliances and storage must have a clear front, with no wall between the resident and the object. Click placed furniture and choose Rotate or Move. The green arrow shows the front in Build mode.

Daily life: click a fridge or cooker for a full meal routine. Add a table and chair for seated eating, and a dishwasher or sink for cleanup. Beds, pools and TVs have animated routines. Adults can take the carpool to work. Welcome a child to grow a new generation; turn aging off to freeze ages.

Save: autosaves every 20 seconds to this browser. These original characters, objects and music are not EA/The Sims assets. This is an early playable sandbox, not the complete Sims 1 game.
